How a PolicyKestrel review runs

  1. Upload the policies

    Drop in the expiring policy and the renewal, or two quotes. Scanned pages are read with Textract.

  2. Read the comparison

    Within minutes each clause sits beside its match, with changed limits, wording and exclusions marked.

  3. Check and send the report

    A broker reviews the marked clauses, adds a note and sends the client a one-page summary.

PolicyKestrel’s platform on AWS

The review platform we are building. The public demo calls none of these services.

Questions

What brokers and risk teams ask before a review.

Ask about a review

Which documents can it read?

Policy wordings, schedules, endorsements and quotes, as digital or scanned PDFs.

Does it recommend a policy?

No. It shows what each policy says and what changed. The broker advises the client.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by how many documents you review a month. We quote after the first review.

How long does a comparison take?

A few minutes for two 60-page policies, then the broker’s own check.

Where are the documents kept?

In an encrypted AWS store for your brokerage alone, deleted on request.
