# Product

PolicyKestrel: Bring clarity to insurance decisions.

AI document intelligence software that helps insurance professionals extract coverage details, compare policies, identify exclusions and review renewal changes.

- Founder and CEO: Abdulmalik Lawal
- Co-founder and CTO: Esther Umeh
- Industry: Insurance, Natural Language Processing, FinTech
- Customers: Insurance brokers, insurance companies and corporate risk departments
- Core features: Insurance document extraction, coverage comparison, exclusion identification, policy renewal summaries, document review dashboards
- MVP: PDF uploads and professionally reviewed policy comparison reports
- Planned AWS: Textract, Bedrock, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda
- Business model: Document-volume subscriptions
- Main action: Request a review
